## Known issues / caveats

- Triangle wave at MIDI note >= 124: the port plays a sine slot where the original reads past its table (documented).
- Bit-exactness is for the 8-bit samples **before** the filter, with the sequencer clock off in the reference.
- The PC reference is a modified build of the original (list in `port/ref/README.md`), not the AVR binary.
- Engine footprint (from the host build): ~34 KB tables in flash, ~3 KB RAM (`shr_v[NPART][NVOICE]`, 96 B/voice)
  — not checked against the FM-1's real budget (Felucca keeps `tests/cpu_baseline.txt` / `target_budget.txt`; a new
  engine may need entries there, UNVERIFIED).
- `fe/eng_shruthi.c` includes `shruthi_core.c` directly (single translation unit, like Felucca's own engines):
  possible symbol clashes with Felucca's firmware TU are untested (it compiled clean next to `dsp.c` only).
- Licensing of a combined firmware must be checked by the owner (Shruthi GPL-3.0-or-later; Felucca read as GPL-3.0-only).
